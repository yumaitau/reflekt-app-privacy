Privacy Policy

Effective 20 August 2026. Last updated: 20 August 2026.

This policy explains how Yuma IT Pty Ltd (ABN 62 684 389 839) ("we", "us", "our") handles personal information collected through Reflekt and this privacy page. We are bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Reflekt is a paused consumer app for collecting daily camera memories. This page is the public privacy notice for that product.

1. Who we are

Yuma IT Pty Ltd is the entity responsible for personal information collected through Reflekt and this page. If you have a question about this policy or about how we handle your information, contact us at hello@yumait.com.au.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to provide Reflekt and to answer you when you contact us.

  • Contact details you give us: name, email address, and anything you include when you write to hello@yumait.com.au.
  • Usage data on this page: this policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the page requested.
  • Camera data: photos and videos captured through your device's camera for the sole purpose of collecting and storing your daily memories.

We do not collect payment card details through Reflekt or this page. We do not collect sensitive information as defined by the Privacy Act, and we ask that you do not send it to us.

3. Camera usage

Our application requests access to your device's camera exclusively for the purpose of helping you collect and store your daily memories. These images and videos:

  • Are stored securely on your device and/or your personal cloud storage
  • Are never shared with third parties for any marketing, advertising, or data mining purposes
  • Are not distributed, sold, or used for any purpose other than providing you with the memory collection service
  • Remain under your complete control at all times

Important note: Your daily memory photos and videos captured through the camera are NEVER shared, distributed, or transmitted to any third parties under any circumstances. These memories remain completely private and are stored only for your personal use.

4. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • provide and maintain Reflekt, including camera memory collection on your device;
  • respond when you contact us about the app or this policy;
  • protect this page and the app from abuse; and
  • meet our legal and record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your information to build advertising profiles, and we do not disclose it for another organisation's marketing.

5. Direct marketing

If you have contacted us about Reflekt, we may reply about that request and about closely related Yuma services. Every such message includes a way to opt out, and we will stop on request. You can opt out at any time by replying to any message from us, or by emailing hello@yumait.com.au.

Visiting this page does not mean you consent to marketing.

6. Who we share it with

We disclose personal information only where we need to run this page or meet the law:

  • GitHub hosts this privacy page on GitHub Pages. A visit may create a technical log on GitHub's systems.
  • Professional advisers (legal, accounting or insurance) where relevant.

We may also disclose information where required or authorised by Australian law, or to protect the rights or safety of any person. If our business or the Reflekt product is sold or restructured, information may transfer to the acquirer, who would remain bound by this policy or one materially equivalent.

Camera memories are not shared with those providers.

7. Overseas disclosure

This privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub is a United States company. Technical logs for visits to this page are likely to be handled in the United States, and may also be handled in other countries where GitHub operates.

We do not operate a separate analytics product on this page. We do not send camera memories overseas, because we do not receive a copy of them.

Where information is handled outside Australia we take reasonable steps to ensure the recipient handles it consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles. We remain accountable under APP 8 for that disclosure. If the hosting location of your data matters, contact us and we will confirm the current position in writing.

8. How we hold and protect it

Contact messages are held in our email inbox. Camera memories are stored on your device and/or your personal cloud storage, not on systems we operate. This page is served from GitHub Pages.

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information we hold from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Those steps include limiting staff access to the inbox we use for this product, and using encrypted connections to this page.

No online service can promise perfect security. If an eligible data breach occurs (unauthorised access, disclosure or loss that is likely to cause serious harm), we will notify affected people and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as soon as practicable, as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme. We do not notify every security incident.

9. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only while we need it, then delete or de-identify it.

  • Camera memories: held on your device and/or your personal cloud. We do not keep a copy. You control how long they remain there.
  • Emails and support messages: retained for 24 months from last contact, unless we must keep them longer.
  • Technical logs on GitHub Pages: retained only as long as GitHub retains hosting logs.
  • Records we must keep by law: retained for that reason alone, even if you have asked us to delete them, and we will tell you when that applies.

Because the consumer app is paused, we are not opening new Reflekt accounts. If we still hold an older contact record, the same rules apply.

10. Accessing and correcting your information

You may ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it is wrong. You may also ask us to delete information we are not required to keep. Email hello@yumait.com.au and we will respond within 30 days.

There is no charge to make a request. We may ask you to verify your identity before we act on one. If we refuse a request we will tell you why in writing, and explain how to complain about that decision.

For camera memories stored only on your device or personal cloud, you already control access, correction and deletion there.

11. Cookies

This page does not set advertising cookies and does not permit third parties to track you across other websites through Reflekt. GitHub Pages may use cookies that are necessary to host and protect the page. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

12. Children

Reflekt is a consumer memory app. It is not directed at children. The Privacy Act protects personal information regardless of age. Capacity to consent is assessed case by case. A person under 18 has capacity if they have enough understanding and maturity to understand what is proposed; otherwise a parent or guardian may act for them.

If it is not practicable to assess capacity, we presume a person aged 15 or over has capacity, unless something suggests otherwise, and we presume a person under 15 does not. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child who does not have capacity unless a parent or guardian is involved. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Complaints

If you think we have breached the Australian Privacy Principles, email hello@yumait.com.au with the details. We will acknowledge your complaint within five business days and give you a written response within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-complaints, or by phone on 1300 363 992.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product or this page changes. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it last changed. If a change materially affects how we handle your personal information, we will take reasonable steps to tell you before it takes effect.
